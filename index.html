Game Rules

⚜ Honour System: This game is for fun, run on the honour system. Please play fair and have fun.
The One Rule: Playtime must be time spent actively attempting to get to orgasm or edge. The one exception is 🌶️ Tease mode — see Session Modes below. Don't game the system with the timer running while you do other things.

How It Works

  • Open the app and start a timer when you play
  • End the timer when you're done — your minutes go on the leaderboard
  • Claim tasks when they pop up for bonus points
  • Each day, the top 3 by playtime earn bonus points — and the leaderboard toppers earn bigger bonuses at the end of the week
  • Most playtime wins overall

Now you know how to play — go play.
Or keep reading for more details.

Session Modes

  • 🔒 Denial — sessions where you're staying on the edge and not allowed to come. Track edges as you go.
  • 💦 Orgasm — sessions where orgasm is allowed/expected. Track orgasms as you go.
  • 🌶️ Tease — toy use during normal activities, not actively trying to come or edge (e.g. wearing a vibe while doing chores). Tease time is kept entirely separate: it doesn't contribute to Overall playtime, Denial/Orgasm Time, Toy Time, Edge or Orgasm Count, or daily awards. It only counts toward the dedicated Toy Tease leaderboard.

🎮 Toy use stacks on top of Denial or Orgasm sessions and feeds the Toy Time leaderboard. A Tease session always requires a toy by design.

Switching Modes

You can pick a different mode every session — there's no commitment. Started a denial session and changed your mind? Just save it and start a new orgasm one. Or switch the mode at save time before submitting.

Tasks

Throughout the day, tasks pop up at random times in the Tasks tab. First come, first served — when you claim a task, no one else can grab it. You have a limited window (typically 1 hour, set by admins) to complete it, then it returns to the pool. You can't reclaim the same instance you gave up on, but the same task template can spawn again later. Once complete, points are added to your tally.

Tasks spawn at random times across the day. If you're asleep or offline when one appears, someone else may grab it — that's just how the timing works. Different people will catch different tasks; over the week it tends to even out.

Posting proof is encouraged where possible in the server, but the app trusts you. Admins can revoke points if foul play is suspected.

Tasks are tagged either 🌐 General or ⛓️ BDSM. By default you only see General tasks. To see and claim BDSM tasks, use the Unlock BDSM toggle at the top of the Tasks tab. You can hide them again anytime. Your BDSM preference is stored on your device only and not visible to other players. The spawner also ensures at least one General task is always available, so opting out never leaves you with nothing to do.

Daily Bonus Points

Every 24 hours (counted from when the game started, so the window is the same for every timezone), the top 3 players by Denial+Orgasm playtime in that window get bonus points. Tease time is excluded from the daily ranking, in line with the Tease rule above.

A session counts toward the window it was saved into, based on when you press End/Save. If your session straddles a window boundary (e.g. starts before midnight, ends after), the whole thing lands in whichever window you saved in. Plan accordingly if you're close to a window flip.

End-of-Game Bonus Points

When the game ends, the top 3 players on each leaderboard get bonus points added to their tally:

  • Overall leaderboard — biggest bonus, awarded after all the others have been calculated so the rankings reflect the final point totals
  • Denial Time, Orgasm Time, Toy Time, Edge Count, Orgasm Count — sub-board bonus tier
  • 🌶️ Toy Tease Time — its own bonus tier (since Tease time doesn't count anywhere else)
  • 🎮 Toy Popularity board does not award points — it's just for fun

The Timer

The timer keeps running even if you close the page or lock your phone (it's tracked on your device). Come back any time to End or Pause it. Pause holds the time still while you step away. If you leave a timer running for over 24 hours, the app assumes you forgot and prompts you to confirm or discard on next visit.

Manual Logs

Couldn't use the timer? You can self-report a session up to 2 hours per entry. Logging unrealistic amounts or repeatedly to inflate your numbers is gaming the system — admins audit and can revoke points or sessions.

Admin Actions

Admins can spawn extra tasks, award or deduct points (with optional reason, kept private), edit or delete sessions, ban users, and transfer points to the main monthly leaderboard. Manual point adjustments aren't shown to other players in the activity log — they just show up silently in totals.

About this app

  • Your timer state and BDSM tasks preference are stored on your device only — no third-party tracking, no analytics.
  • Sessions, claimed tasks, and points are stored on the game's database and visible to other players in the game.
  • Once the game ends, all data will be deleted.
  • This is a community game running on the honour system. Be kind to each other.
